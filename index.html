Our Story

Forty Years of Family, Craft & Trust

Founded on a Simple Mission. In the 1990s, Shree Himmat Mal Banthia saw a clear need: schools needed uniforms that would last through an entire academic year, fit properly across all sizes, and arrive on time. He built his workshop around this mission—quality over shortcuts, relationships over volume. For four decades, this commitment to meticulous craftsmanship became the foundation that schools, retailers, and wholesalers across India trusted.

Evolution with Purpose. When Amit Banthia established Classroom Uniforms in 2018, he didn't abandon what made the family business special. Instead, he evolved it. We kept the in-house cutting, rigorous quality checks, and direct relationships—the same precision that defined four decades. What's new: modern technology, specialized expertise, and professional workflows that let us scale responsibly while maintaining integrity at every step.

From Classroom to the World. The standards that made us trusted with school uniforms extend to everything we create. Today, we serve schools, retailers, wholesalers, corporate clients, and hospitality businesses with the same reliability, durability, and attention to detail built over four decades. Every uniform reflects our heritage and our commitment to excellence.

How We Work

From Fabric to Finished Uniform

Every order moves through our seven-stage process, cut and quality-checked at every step — even the parts we hand to trusted stitching partners for extra capacity.

  1. 1
    Step 01

    Fabric Sourcing

    Durable, pre-shrunk cotton blends and easy-care fabrics, chosen specifically to survive a full school term of daily wear and washing.

  2. 2
    Step 02

    Pattern & Cutting

    Patterns are graded per school across every size and cut in-house, so a Class 1 shirt and a Class 10 shirt fit the same way, year after year.

  3. 3
    Step 03

    Stitching & Assembly

    Reinforced seams, proper interlining on collars and blazers, and construction built for daily wear. Stitched in our own workshop, and by trusted stitching partners we personally oversee when order volumes call for extra capacity.

  4. 4
    Step 04

    Embroidery & Labeling

    Embroidery, badges, crests and labels are added to each school's own specification, colours and branding requirements.

  5. 5
    Step 05

    Quality Check

    Every finished piece is checked for stitching, sizing and fabric consistency before it's cleared to be packed.

  6. 6
    Step 06

    Finishing & Pressing

    Final inspection, pressing and packaging of finished uniforms to ensure they arrive pristine and ready to wear.

  7. 7
7
Step 07

Packing & Bulk Dispatch

Orders are sorted by size and class, then dispatched on a schedule planned around the academic calendar — so they arrive before term starts.
